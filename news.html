Three gold medals for GD Goenka Public School at the CBSE skating competition

Rudransh Sharma won two gold medals and Prisha Ojha won one at the CBSE East Zone Roller Skating Tournament, held in Lucknow, bringing three golds to GD Goenka Public School, Dhanbad. Principal Heena Parveen and the management congratulated both skaters and thanked their coaches and parents.

Oct 2026 · Hindi daily newspaper

Four Dhanbad skaters selected in the SGFI state selection trials

From the 70th SGFI State Level Open Trials 2026-27 at Khelgaon, Ranchi: Hargun Kaur and Navya Seth (U-14 Girls Inline), Rudransh Sharma (U-17 Boys Inline) and Prisha Ojha (U-17 Girls Inline) have been selected for the Jharkhand state team. Next come the state camp and then the national SGFI competition. Five Dhanbad skaters took part; coach Abhishek Kumar credited regular practice and discipline.
