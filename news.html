Three gold medals for GD Goenka Public School at the CBSE skating competition

Rudransh Sharma won two gold medals and Prisha Ojha won one at the CBSE East Zone Roller Skating Tournament, held in Lucknow, bringing three golds to GD Goenka Public School, Dhanbad. Principal Heena Parveen and the management congratulated both skaters and thanked their coaches and parents.

Oct 2026 · Hindi daily newspaper

Four Dhanbad skaters selected in the SGFI state selection trials

From the 70th SGFI State Level Open Trials 2026-27 at Khelgaon, Ranchi: Hargun Kaur and Navya Seth (U-14 Girls Inline), Rudransh Sharma (U-17 Boys Inline) and Prisha Ojha (U-17 Girls Inline) have been selected for the Jharkhand state team. Next come the state camp and then the national SGFI competition. Five Dhanbad skaters took part; coach Abhishek Kumar credited regular practice and discipline.

Aug 2025 · G D Goenka Public School, Dhanbad

Dhanbad skaters win medals at the CBSE Zonal Skating Championship

The CBSE Zonal Skating Championship 2025 was held on the home rink at G D Goenka Public School, Dhanbad, across 3 states, with 1500+ races and 1200+ skaters. Dhanbad medalists: Rudransh Sharma (2 gold), Navya Seth (1 bronze), Prisha Ojha (2 silver) and Maanvik Raaj Bhushan (1 gold, 1 silver).

Jun 2025 · DDRSA

Navya Barnwal wins silver at the 9th Ranking Open National Championship in Kolkata

Navya Barnwal won the silver medal in the 1 Lap Road Race (Quad category) in the 6 to 8 years age group at the 9th Ranking Open National Speed Skating Championship in Kolkata, held in June 2025. Family and association statement: she is the first girl from Jharkhand to win a national-level medal in speed skating.

Feb 2025 · DDRSA

Asian bronze medalist Dhanush Babu trains Dhanbad skaters

Dhanush Babu, an Asian bronze medalist, held a training camp for Dhanbad skaters from 15 to 21 February 2025. The camp covered refining skating skills, race strategies, sprint enhancement, and diet and physiotherapy.
