Welcome

About DDRSA

Dhanbad District Roller Skating Association (DDRSA) is the district body for roller skating, with speed skating at its core, affiliated with the Roller Skating Association of Jharkhand. We register skaters and clubs, train coaches and referees, run district championships and select the district team.
