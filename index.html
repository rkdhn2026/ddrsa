The CBSE Zonal Skating Championship 2025 was held on the home rink at G D Goenka Public School, Dhanbad, across 3 states, with 1500+ races and 1200+ skaters. Dhanbad medalists: Rudransh Sharma (2 gold), Navya Seth (1 bronze), Prisha Ojha (2 silver) and Maanvik Raaj Bhushan (1 gold, 1 silver).

Jun 2025 · DDRSA

Navya Barnwal wins silver at the 9th Ranking Open National Championship in Kolkata

Navya Barnwal won the silver medal in the 1 Lap Road Race (Quad category) in the 6 to 8 years age group at the 9th Ranking Open National Speed Skating Championship in Kolkata, held in June 2025. Family and association statement: she is the first girl from Jharkhand to win a national-level medal in speed skating.

Welcome

About DDRSA

Dhanbad District Roller Skating Association (DDRSA) is the district body for roller skating, with speed skating at its core. We register skaters and clubs, train coaches and referees, run district championships and select the district team.

Read more →

New to skating?

Join a beginner batch at an affiliated club. Bring water, comfortable clothes and a helmet if you have one. Clubs lend skates for the first month.
