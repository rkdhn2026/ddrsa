National championship

Dhanbad skaters race at the 12th National Ranking championship

Skaters from Dhanbad joined Ranchi, Jamshedpur and Bokaro at the national ranking open speed skating championship at the new Khelgaon rink in Ranchi. Rudransh Sharma won three golds (200 m Time Trial, 500 m and 1 Lap Road), Uttam Kumar Rajwar won two silvers (3000 m Road and 500 m D) and Sharanaya Upadhyaa won bronze in the 4 Lap Rink event.

Welcome

About DDRSA

Dhanbad District Roller Skating Association (DDRSA) is the district body for roller skating, with speed skating at its core. We register skaters and clubs, train coaches and referees, run district championships and select the district team.

Read more →

New to skating?

Join a beginner batch at an affiliated club. Bring water, comfortable clothes and a helmet if you have one. Clubs lend skates for the first month.
