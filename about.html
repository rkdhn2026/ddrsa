About DDRSA

Who we are

Dhanbad District Roller Skating Association (DDRSA) is the district governing body for roller skating in Dhanbad district, Jharkhand, affiliated with the Roller Skating Association of Jharkhand and national federation.

MissionOffice bearers & committeeAffiliated clubsPolicies

Our mission

To make speed skating accessible to every child and adult in the district, and to give talented skaters a fair, clear path from their first session to the national podium.

DDRSA was formed in 2023 and now has 12 affiliated clubs.

What we do

  • Annual registration of skaters and clubs
  • District championships, ranking races and selection trials
  • Coach and referee education
  • Team selection for state and national championships

Prevention of age fraud

Every skater's age is checked against a birth certificate at registration. Entries in the wrong category are disqualified and results withdrawn.

Safe sport

Helmets and protective gear are compulsory at all DDRSA events. Coaches follow the federation safeguarding code.
