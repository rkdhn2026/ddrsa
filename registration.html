Annual registration 2026-27

Skater registration

Registration is mandatory for every skater in club, district, state and national events. It is valid from 1 April 2026 to 31 March 2027.

How it worksRegisterSkater login

How it works

  • Fill in the form below. Your race category is worked out from your date of birth.
  • Pay the registration fee of ₹ amount at your club or online.
  • Your club verifies your birth certificate and photo.
  • You get a DDRSA skater ID to use for every event entry.

Documents needed

  • Birth certificate (government-issued)
  • Recent passport-size photo
  • Aadhaar or school ID of the skater
  • Parent's consent for skaters under 18
