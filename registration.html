Annual registration 2026-27

Register for 2026-27

Skaters and coaches register with DDRSA every season. Registration is mandatory for club, district, state and national events and is valid from 1 April 2026 to 31 March 2027.

SkaterCoach

For skaters

Skater registration

How it works

  • Fill in the form below. Your race category is worked out from your date of birth.
  • Pay the registration fee of ₹ amount at your club or online.
  • Your club verifies your birth certificate and photo.
  • You get a DDRSA skater ID to use for every event entry.

Documents needed

  • Birth certificate (government-issued)
  • Recent passport-size photo
  • Aadhaar or school ID of the skater
  • Parent's consent for skaters under 18

Registration form

Used only by DDRSA to verify the skater's identity and age. It is kept privately and is never shown on the website.

Optional: for the skater's profile

Choose a photo from your phone or computer. It is shrunk automatically and sent with the form. It is only shown on the public profile if you tick the profile box below.

For coaches

Coach registration

What you need

  • Coaching certificate or qualification, if you hold one
  • Government photo ID and a recent passport-size photo
  • First-aid certificate (needed for level 1 and above)
  • Name of the club where you coach
